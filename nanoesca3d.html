ℹ️ How to use (tap to expand)
Load a folder (dld_his_tr_*.dat + its .TIF slices) or press Demo Data.
3D view: drag = rotate · wheel = zoom · right-drag = pan · double-click = reset.
Touch: 1 finger rotate · 2 fingers pinch-zoom + pan · double-tap reset.
Slabs: each of E / kx / ky is a cut plane. Full = whole axis, Plane = single cut, −/+ = band width. Once an axis is on Plane, that cut follows the cursor.
Minimap: shows the kx×ky map at the cursor energy. Drag on it to move the (kx,ky) cursor.
Profiles: drag a profile to move its cursor coordinate; the other two re-slice at the new point.
Colours: drag the two handles on the colour bar to set the min/max shown; double-click the bar to reset. Gamma (<1) lifts faint signal; Percentile sets a robust window (try 2 / 98).
Data: dI/dE shows the energy-derivative (background cancels, edges/bands light up) — good for weak, near-Fermi features.
Keys: ←→ E · ↑↓ kx · [] ky · R reset.
